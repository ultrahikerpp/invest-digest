## Workload Estimate

The nominal range is 633 episode numbers. The actual work count may be lower if an episode was deleted, renumbered, or has no usable source.

- Archive enumeration and manifest validation: approximately 1–4 hours, depending on the approved archive source.
- Transcript acquisition: approximately 1–3 hours when captions are available; Whisper fallback can add roughly 3–8 minutes per episode and is the largest uncertainty.
- ChatGPT summary plus Shorts-card generation: approximately 4–8 minutes per episode at the current observed browser pace, excluding deployment.
- For the 528 reachable YouTube episodes, AI/card runtime is approximately 35–70 hours. Including transcript fallback, validation, cooldowns, and grouped deployments, plan for roughly 45–100 hours of total machine time.
- Safe wall-clock schedule: 12–24 episodes per day, or roughly 4–8 weeks. A more aggressive 24–36 episodes per day may finish in roughly 3–4 weeks but increases ChatGPT rate-limit risk.

The estimate should be recalculated after a four-episode archive pilot measures caption coverage, average transcript length, and ChatGPT response time.

## Recommended Scheduling Strategy

1. Run one manifest/ingestion pilot for EP631–EP633 plus one lower-range control episode. Do not start the full archive before the manifest and transcript coverage report are complete.
2. Process sequentially in 3-episode ChatGPT batches, with one browser session and no parallel ChatGPT calls.
3. Leave a 30–60 minute cooldown between batches initially. Increase the daily batch count only after two consecutive days without login, Cloudflare, or rate-limit errors.
4. Use SQLite status and per-episode files as checkpoints. A failed batch stops immediately; the next run resumes only from the explicitly confirmed episode.
5. Deploy after every 3 successful batches (9 episodes), rather than after every 3-episode batch. The bounded `--no-deploy` option keeps the current runner workflow and defers deployment without changing summary/card generation.
6. Perform a nightly verification: required Gooaye headings, no browser artifacts such as `Thinking`, all card PNGs readable, CTA present, amber top-strip color, SQLite `done / processed=1`, and no EP634+ rows changed.
